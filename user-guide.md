# 📘🌸 User Guide & Onboarding — Personal Health Dashboard 💖✨

## 📌 🎀 Welcome!

Welcome to your new digital wellness companion! This guide will help you set up, customize, and navigate your **Personal Health & Analytics Dashboard** so you can easily track your progress in style. 🌷💕

---

## 🚀 📋 Step 1: Getting Started

1. Open the [Google Sheets Template Link](https://www.google.com/).
2. Click **File ➔ Make a copy** to save the spreadsheet directly to your personal Google Drive. 💾✨
3. Rename your file to something cute and personal (e.g., *My Wellness Journey 2026* 🌸).

---

## 🎯 ⚙️ Step 2: Configure Your Goals

Before logging your daily data, head over to the **`03 — Goals & Settings`** tab:

* Enter your starting metrics and target goals (weight, daily step goals, sleep targets, and calorie baselines). 🎯
* These parameters power all the automated milestone trackers and progress bars across your dashboard. ✨

---

## 📝 🌿 Step 3: Daily Logging

Every day, open the **`02 — Daily Log`** tab and fill out your latest entries:

* Log your morning weight, calorie intake, steps, hours of sleep, and daily mood. ☕️💤
* **Pro Tip:** Leave cells blank for days you choose not to log—the built-in error handling formulas (`IF` / `ISBLANK`) ensure your charts and rolling averages stay clean without breaking! 🛡️📊

---

## 📊 ✨ Step 4: Explore Your Dashboard & Analytics

Once your data is flowing, switch back and forth between your tabs to watch the magic happen:

* **`01 — Dashboard`** ➔ View your executive summary cards, current streaks, and visual progress toward your milestones (`✨ Completed`, `⏳ Pending`, `🎉 Achieved!`). 🏆💖
* **`04 — Analytics`** ➔ Review deeper statistical breakdowns and trends over time. 📈🧠
* **`05 — Habit Tracker`** ➔ Monitor lifestyle consistency, mood distributions, and habit completion rates. 🌿🎨
